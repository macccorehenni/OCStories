# The Stars Shine Still

_Writer's note: While set in the Multi-world, this is not cannon to it, but the Tales of Aeverath. In terms of Rainbow Bridge, this is arc 2.5— between "Anti-Gods".

 This happens after the main chapter in the group story.

It was hopeless. While she had just figured out that her friend — no, her _girlfriend_— was her partner for life, now she was gone. Anna considered going back up on that roof, but decided, _no_, she wanted to live. For her. She would have to be strong to protect her domain— she would _not_ fall to the Shadow. She had saved the world twice— but with a pang of grief, she recalled that she did it with the one she would spend the rest of eternity— literally— with, who was now gone. The Shadow— helmed, at least here in Celestial Danae, by the same Dark Shadow who she thought that she had taken down— threatened to take her too. With Umbra and Irindel, as well as most of their siblings, and Lisanna too, succumbed to this shadow, that now threatened to take over Celestial Danae. But then, as she sulked, she remembered— she had people, and _they didn't know_. She ran, as fast as she could, to the portal room.

"Anna, nice to see you! Where's Celeste?" Maccore smiled, but his smile faded when he saw the look of terror, plain as day.

"The Shadow reached Celestial Danae, didn't it?"

Anna, in tears, could do no better than nod.
