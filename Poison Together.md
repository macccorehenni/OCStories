# The Stars Shine Still

_Writer's note: While set in Celestial Danae, this should not be taken as canon to the Rainbow Bridge plane's own canon, but that of the Tales of Aeverath. In the canon of RB, this takes place between "Anti-Gods" and "Celestial Family"._

It was hopeless. While she had just figured out that her friend — no, her _girlfriend_— was her partner for life, now she was gone. Anna considered going back up on that roof, but decided, _no_, she wanted to live. For her. She would have to be strong to protect her domain— she would _not_ fall to the Shadow. She had saved the world once— but with a pang of grief, she recalled that she did it with the one she would spend the rest of eternity— literally— with. The Shadow— helmed, at least here in Celestial Danae, by the same Dark Shadow who she thought that she had taken down— threatened to take her too.
